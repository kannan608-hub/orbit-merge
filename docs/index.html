Privacy Policy for Orbit Merge

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data Orbit Merge ("the app") collects and how it is used. Orbit Merge is a single-player puzzle game developed by an independent developer.

Data we collect directly

Orbit Merge does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. The app stores your best score locally on your device (using local storage) so it persists between sessions. This score is never transmitted off your device and we have no access to it.

Data collected by third-party services

Orbit Merge displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your device's Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completions)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can review and adjust your ad personalization settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Your choices

On iOS, you will be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking-based advertising identifier is used; you may decline this at any time in your device's Settings.

On Android and iOS, you can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on Android; Settings → Privacy → Tracking, on iOS).

Children's privacy

Orbit Merge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take steps to remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not operate any server or database that stores your personal data. Your best score exists only in your device's local storage and is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
